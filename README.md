# 🛍️ Boutique Sales Automation

**Built by XU Automations | More automation. Less repetition.**

##  Project Overview

Boutique Sales Automation is an n8n workflow project designed to help boutique owners and online clothing vendors organize sales records, track inventory information, and receive important business updates.

The goal is to reduce repetitive administrative work and make everyday business operations easier to manage.

##  The Problem

Many boutique owners manage their businesses manually, which can involve:

* Recording customer orders and sales.
* Updating inventory after purchases.
* Checking available stock.
* Organizing sales records.
* Keeping track of business activity.

These tasks can consume valuable time and make records harder to maintain as a business grows.

##  The Solution

This project explores how workflow automation can connect sales information, inventory records, and business notifications.

The intended workflow can include:

1. Receiving sales information.
2. Processing and organizing sales records.
3. Tracking inventory changes.
4. Preparing relevant business updates.
5. Notifying the business owner when configured conditions are met.

The exact features depend on the workflow implementation and integrations.

##  Tools & Technologies

* **n8n:** Workflow automation.
* **Google Sheets:** Structured data storage, where configured.
* **AI integrations:** Optional, depending on the use case.

##  Workflow Overview

**Sales Information → Data Processing → Sales Records → Inventory Tracking → Business Updates**

This is a high-level illustration of the intended process, not a claim that every step is already implemented.

##  Expected Benefits

* Less repetitive data entry.
* Better-organized sales records.
* Easier inventory tracking.
* More consistent business updates.
* More time for customer service and business growth.

Actual results will depend on the final implementation and how the business uses the workflow.
